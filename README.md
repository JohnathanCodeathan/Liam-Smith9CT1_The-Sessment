## What even is this:
This is a data analysis program designed to help users analyse the data that I've collected about whether people can survive on minimum wage in Australia now, compared to back then.
## THE RULES:
1. Read the whole readme file before using the program, its very helpful.
2. Listen to what the program tells you to, it's telling you that for a reason.
3. Don't enter a blank value, that's just silly, the program doesn't want that
4. When it asks you to enter a number, enter a number. When it asks you to enter a number with a decimal, enter a number with a decimal, like 21.01
5. I think thats it for now

## Helpful tidbits:
- The data goes from the year 2000 to 2026
- The datas columns are in this order: 
  1. year: The year
  2. minin_$: The weekly minimum wage of that year 
  3. groceries_$: The average weekly grocery price of that year 
  4. house_$: The average weekly rent price of that year 
  5. electricity_$: The average weekly electricity bill of that year 
  6. water_$: The average weekly water bill of that year 
  7. costtot_$: The total weekly expenses of that year, just columns 3-6 added up 
  8. savtot_$: The total weekly amount people save for that year, just minimum wage minus the total expenses 
  9. todminin_$: Weekly minimum wage of that year, but adjusted for inflation,
  10. todcostot_$: The total expenses but adjusted for inflation
  11. todsav_$: The total savings but adjusted for inflation
